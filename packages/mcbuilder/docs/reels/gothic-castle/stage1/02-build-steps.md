# Stage 1b Build steps: OMGGamer Gothic Castle reel (church/cathedral grammar)

Video: `~/workspace/gothic-castle-reel/gothic-castle.mp4`, 14.6 s, 1080x1920 @30fps. Frames: 15 extracts at 1 fps in `frames-1fps/` (f_001-f_015). All 15 frames read directly for this file, plus zoomed crops of the tower top, the chandelier/graveyard area, and the rose window.

**Numbers convention:** every dimension below is measured from the frames and marked ~ (approximate, ±1 block) for small measurements. Large heights are given as ranges (for example tower 45-55), because perspective makes ±1 impossible at that scale. Nothing here is block-counted from a schematic, there is none. Where a number is NOT resolvable, it says so; no number is invented to fill a gap.

## Method and camera caveat

- Each step below names what is NEW vs the previous frame. Tags: **VISIBLE** = directly seen in the frames; **INFERRED** = order deduced (for example walls must precede the roof frame that sits on them, even though both appear in the same 1-second interval).
- **The camera orbits and jumps.** f_001 is front-left, f_002 swings to the front, f_003 to the side, f_004 to the rear/top, f_005 to the rear, f_006 to the tower front, f_007 frontal, f_008/f_009 to the side wing, f_010/f_011 top-down, f_012 to the rear, f_013-f_015 showcase orbits. An element "new" in a frame that sits on a face hidden in the previous frame may have been built earlier; where that applies, it is flagged. f_004 and f_005 show the SAME rose screen wall from front and back; they are one state, not two steps.
- **1-second granularity limit:** at 1 fps, "appears in the same interval" proves nothing about intent or sub-order inside that second. Two elements appearing together may have been built in either order within that second.
- **The video starts mid-build.** At f_001 the platform, stair, and columns are already up. The footprint to platform transition is NOT observable; only its traces survive.
- **Fidelity caveat (see 01):** this video is an edited composite, not a faithful chronological timelapse. Evidence: chandeliers hanging from chains out of frame with no support (f_002/f_003); the tower crown flat-topped in f_013 but spired in f_014/f_015; the tower upper story absent in f_007 but present in f_013-f_015; the panel below the rose flat in f_012 but gridded in f_013/f_014. First-appearance order is therefore a reconstruction with low confidence for the tower crown and the chandeliers. Where the video's apparent order conflicts with a sane build order, this file gives the sane order and says so.
- No interior shots, no dusk showcase. Interior furnishing and lighting steps are out of scope except the chandeliers, which are visible through the open wing roof in f_008 and are recorded as observed, with their construction flagged unresolvable.

## Mode assumption

The sequence is written as if built in SURVIVAL (access matters): the grand stair goes in first so the builder can walk up instead of pillaring on dirt, and every step above ground level carries an access note (scaffold / dirt pillar / the stair itself). In creative with flight the same order still works, but the access notes are unnecessary.

**Livable minimum (A17):** the video's order (arrival first, then walls, then roofs) leaves the follower with no livable stopping point until the nave is roofed. The sane follower order is: platform + stair, then nave walls + roof (first livable point: a closed, lit shell you can sleep in and store things in), then the towers, then the wing, then the pavilion dressing and graveyard last. The steps below keep the video's observed order for the record, but each step names the livable stopping point it reaches. Stopping points: after Step 9 the nave is roofed (livable, once torches or lanterns are placed inside); before that, nothing is livable (open walls, no roof, mobs can walk in). Do not stop overnight at f_003's state (tall walls, glazed windows, no roof).

**Permanent access (A17):** leave a stairwell slot ~3-4 x 1 in the nave floor deck above the internal stair top, or the upper floors become unreachable from inside (the guide's own rule, quoted from the Tall Mansion fold). No internal stair, ladder, or deck opening is visible in the reel; the follower must add one. The towers need an internal stair or ladder too: f_006 shows an arched doorway under the tower window and f_013-f_015 show a tall tower with an upper story, but no internal route is ever shown.

## The build, step by step

- **INFERRED:** the site is chosen, not flattened, the build sits ON the grass at the hill's foot (T8 siting: a clearing at a terrain foot, forest as backdrop). Whether the clearing was flattened or found is not resolvable.
- **Access:** ground level, no access needed.

### Step 1 Raised platform + grand stair + 4 columns (f_001)
- **VISIBLE (f_001):** a raised pale-stone platform (~12 x ~10, ~6-8 above grade) with a grand stair (~10 wide, ~17 treads visible in zoom, run ~15-18) running down to the grass, and 4 tall pale columns standing on the platform (4 columns in 2 pairs flanking the front, ~8-10 tall from the deck, with cap blocks). A low parapet runs along the BACK edge of the platform only (white fence course, not all edges). The platform's side face shows a pale block grid (stone-brick pattern).
- **INFERRED:** the stair and platform are the footprint, the build's ground plan is declared by the platform, not by a drawn outline. No outline is visible anywhere in this reel. Whether the platform is solid fill (~720 blocks) or hollow walls + deck (~380) is not visible; build it hollow. The ~17 treads do not reconcile with a ~6-8 rise at 1 block per tread, so the steps are most likely slabs (~0.5 rise each) on a solid sloped base, but the construction is NOT resolvable; build the stair to match your platform height.
- **Access:** the stair itself is the access, walk up it; no scaffold needed.
- **Stage tags:** as-built footprint/grid + ground connection; locked footprint/grid (agree) but the stair is arrival detail built FIRST, before any walls (see deviation D1).

## Verdict: does the observed order follow the locked staged process?

**No at the macro level, and the finding is about the locked process, not about OMGGamer. Third data point, same verdict as Tall Mansion.**

The locked process (2026-11-01, amended 2026-11-07) is: footprint, signature element alone, skeleton, shell, detail exterior, interior (only after shell closed), ruin, landscape last, with the amendment that stage discipline applies per volume and signature-first applies when the build's identity is a single volume. This reel is a multi-volume build (platform + pavilion + nave + two rose volumes + wing), so the amendment predicts per-volume discipline with the signature mid-sequence, and that is what is observed. What the reel actually does: platform + stair, pavilion, nave walls, rose wall, east volume front, east volume spire, wing + chandeliers, wing roof layer, nave roof, twin frame, second rose tower, showcase. The build alternates between volumes instead of finishing one stage cleanly before the next, and the signature is neither first nor finished in one pass.

Findings, explicitly:

- **D1 Arrival first.** The grand stair + raised platform + columns are built before any walls (Step 1, f_001). The locked process starts with footprint/grid, and the platform IS the footprint, but the stair is arrival detail (technique 11/40 territory) built first. The arrival is the build's first statement, not its last.
- **D2 Exterior detail interleaved with shell (classification, not deviation).** The entrance pavilion (Step 2) is built before the nave walls are finished and before any roof exists. If the pavilion is classified as entrance shell rather than "detail exterior", the deviation evaporates, a classification choice, not an observation. Same finding as Tall Mansion D2 (porch).
- **D3 Volumes alternate; the signature is mid-sequence.** The rose wall and east volume spire (Steps 4-6) come after the pavilion and nave walls, and the twin frame and second rose tower (Steps 10-11) come after both roofs. This is exactly what the 2026-11-07 amendment predicts for a multi-volume build, per-volume discipline, signature mid-sequence. The amendment holds on its second test.
- **D4 WITHDRAWN (was: interior lighting installed while the roof is still open).** The old D4 rested on the chandeliers appearing in f_002/f_003, before any roof existed. The roast showed those chandeliers hang from chains that exit the frame with no support, and the fidelity caveat shows the video is an edited composite. A floating chandelier is not a build step, so its timing proves nothing about interior timing. The chandeliers that ARE real (three clusters inside the open wing, f_008) hang where a roof frame will go, and the follower order hangs them after the frame exists. Nothing in this reel contradicts "interior only after shell closed" once the edit artifact is accounted for. The lighting-exception proposal is withdrawn with D4, and NO process change is proposed from this reel on interior timing.
- **D5 Landscape/grounding interleaved, not last.** The graveyard is visible during the wing work (f_008/f_009), not after the whole build. Same finding as Tall Mansion D3 (hedges): landscape material goes in with its volume. In the follower order it moves to after the roofs, because the crosses sit in the roof-work zone.
- **D6 Signature element built during the shell.** The rose screen wall (Step 4) goes up while the nave roof is still an open deck. Under the original locked process the signature goes first and alone; under the amendment it goes per-volume, and here it goes mid-shell, which the amendment permits but does not precisely predict.

What DOES follow the locked process: footprint first (Step 1, as far as the video shows); the shell-before-roof discipline inside each volume (nave: walls, deck, roof; wing: walls, roof layer, roof; east volume: shaft, spire); and each volume is built bottom-up in stages. So the discipline is real, but it lives inside each volume, the macro-order across volumes is the builder's own, not the locked one. After D4 is withdrawn, NO rule of the locked process is still contradicted by this reel. The amendment stands as-is; no new process note is proposed for lock from this reel.
