## 1. Machicolation gallery band — KEEP

**Why it survives:** the projection + opening row + shadow gap is a
composition neither T7 nor T5 gives. T7's parapet is a rim only (alternating
high/low, no projecting course, no openings below); T5's base crenellation
is the same verb at the opposite end with no opening row. The gallery's
novelty is the *hanging* opening row under a *projecting* course.

**Vary-by-level correction — CONFIRMED, and it holds inside single frames,
not just across them:**
- Lower band (stage 1→2): tall pointed arches, 2–4 tall — f0004 (close-up),
  f0006 (both bands in one frame: pointed arches below), f0007, f0013.
- Upper band (stage 2→3): small square murder-holes under the parapet —
  f0006 (same frame as the arches: small square notches under the top
  parapet), f0007, f0009, f0010.
- f0006 is the killer evidence: one frame, both bands, two visibly
  different opening shapes. This is not a resolution artifact. The
  "do NOT copy-paste the pointed-arch row at every transition" guard
  stays in the recipe.
- T15 cross-ref for arch construction is correct — do not re-specify
  arch building inside this technique.

**No change needed for stage 3.** Cite f0004/f0006/f0007 for the
vary-by-level claim.

## 2. Stepped tower staging — KEEP

**Why it survives:** it fills a gap the guide leaves open. T4's compound
hierarchy composes *multiple* towers (flankers + one dominant); T13
governs *narrow* (≤6×6) towers and its own guard says ≥8×8 footprints
should "compose freely instead" — but gives no discipline for that free
composition. Candidate 2 IS that discipline for a single wide tower:
3 stages (2 minimum), each 1–2 narrower per side, each transition
gallery-capped, base stage tallest, top stage narrowest carrying the
crown. The ≥8×8 guard is exactly what keeps it from collapsing into
T13's territory — verified against f0016 (three clear stages + spire,
wide footprint throughout).

**Massing guard fold (from candidate 6) — CONFIRMED as the right home.**
f0016 shows the observation plainly: shaft = noise + slit grid only,
all ornament at transitions + crown. Candidate 2 is the composition
technique, so the guard belongs inside it, not as a standalone entry.

**No change needed for stage 3.** Keep the "never another full stage"
and "max 4 stages" guards — both are reasonable and uncontradicted.

## 3. Stepped dark spire with accent + finial — KEEP

**Why it survives:** T4's spire coverage is pavilion-scale pyramid roofs
(stepped stair roof, slab + finial cap) and a generic "one vertical
accent." The heavy spire is a different object: roughly shaft-height tall
(f0016: spire ≈ stages below it), dark stone against a pale shaft,
stepped taper (f0010: clear 1-inset-per-2–3-rise profile), exactly ONE
red accent block near the tip (f0010/f0012/f0016 — one red block in
every angle, never a band), thin 1×1 finial spike, plus one subordinate
spire at ~60% on a side turret with no accent (f0012/f0016). None of
that is T4's pavilion roof.

**Flags carried into stage 3 (unchanged from v2):** step ratio is
estimated from the stepped profile, not block-counted — keep the word
"roughly" in the recipe. Accent material is red-family only (concrete vs
wool vs redstone block indistinguishable at this resolution) — do not
assert a specific block.

**One stage-3 addition:** candidate 4's arcade folds in here (see below)
as a named sub-section. The spire recipe must NOT describe the taper as
"solid" once the arcade is folded in — say "mass first, arcade second."

## 4. Spire-base arcade — FIX (fold into candidate 3, do not number it)

**The observation is frame-true — this is not a DROP:**
- f0012 shows it unmistakably: a pale stone frame with a double arched
  opening punched into the dark spire's lower third (front face), plus a
  second pale-framed arch on the side face, plus a pale-framed arch at
  the subordinate spire's base.
- f0016 confirms it at wide-shot distance (pale double-arch unit on the
  spire's front face, below the red accent).

**Distinct from the two things it could be confused with:**
- Gallery arches (candidate 1): those hang BELOW a projecting course on
  the shaft stages; the arcade is FLUSH in the spire taper with pale
  surrounds. Different location, different construction.
- T7 windows: no glass, no shutters, no flower box — arched, pale
  surround, in a dark spire, not a wall. T7's domestic recipe is the
  opposite of this.

**Why it still does not earn its own number:** its apply-condition is
parasitic — v2's own recipe says "Apply when: tall dark spires on pale
towers (with candidate 3)." A technique that only ever applies inside
another technique's scope is a detail of that technique, not a
standalone grammar. T15 already covers arch construction; the arcade's
only novel content is "pale surround + in the spire taper," which is a
placement rule for the spire. Numbering it would inflate the guide with
a one-paragraph technique that can never be used alone.

**FIX specification for stage 3:** fold the full arcade recipe into
candidate 3 as a named sub-section ("Spire-base arcade") — keep every
line: lower third only, 2–4 small arched openings (1–2 wide, 2–3 tall),
pale-stone surrounds, spaced around the taper, small-and-few guard
("mass first, arcade second"), short-spire and pale-spire exclusions.
Keep the flag: exact opening count per face not resolvable at distance
(MED-HIGH, not HIGH). Do NOT give it its own technique number.

## 5. Paired slit window grid — KEEP (standalone, not a T7 extension)

**Why standalone holds:** f0013 is the decisive frame — pairs of 1-wide
× 2-tall dark slits, no glass, no shutters, no sills, no lintels,
aligned in a strict grid across the whole face, with pairs between
pilaster ribs on the upper stage (f0007). T7's window recipe is glass
panes + shutters + flower box in vertical columns — candidate 5 inverts
every detail of it. A "variant" of T7 cannot invert T7; that is a
different technique.

**T13 override is legitimate, not a scope violation:** T13's
quiet-shaft corollary (at most one slit per face) governs landmark
shafts whose crown carries the read and ≤6×6 footprints. Candidate 5
is scoped to wide fortress towers only, and T13's own guard explicitly
releases ≥8×8 footprints to free composition — candidate 5 gives that
free composition its discipline. The override is stated, scoped, and
consistent with T13's text.

**Lone single slit (f0013) — correctly handled in v2:** one single slit
sits between pairs mid-face. v2's LOW note ("pairs-dominant, not
pairs-exclusive; a lone slit does not break the grid") is the right
call — do not let stage 3 over-police this into "pairs ONLY."

**No change needed for stage 3.**

## Deliberately rejected takes (checked and discarded)

- "Upper square holes are just the lower arches at lower resolution" —
  rejected: f0006 shows both bands in ONE frame at the same distance,
  and the shapes are visibly different (tall pointed vs small square).
- "Arcade is just T15 arches" — rejected: T15 gives arch construction,
  not the placement rule (pale-framed openings in a dark spire taper
  tying crown to shaft). The placement is novel; it just belongs inside
  candidate 3, not on its own.
- "Slit grid is a T7 variant" — rejected: it inverts every T7 detail
  (see candidate 5). A variant cannot be the opposite of its parent.
- "Staging is T13" — rejected: T13 is ≤6×6-only by its own guard text;
  this tower is wide and its staging grammar (gallery-capped steps) is
  not T13's ornament discipline.
