## Candidate 2 — East-Asian tiled roof grammar: KEEP

- Frame-verified: frame 03 shows dark tile + red + timber eave bands on the
  wall wing; frame 06 shows the dark tile field, timber fascia, and red
  ridge ticks; frame 08 shows the layered eave on the gatehouse and main
  hall.
- Not a T4 duplicate: T4 covers dark stair-block roofs, two-tone striping,

  reads as a subtle 1-block lift at the red hall's corners (frames 06/07),
  not a dramatic sweep. Keep the recipe's own wording ("subtle, never a
  horn") and do not let stage 3 inflate it.
