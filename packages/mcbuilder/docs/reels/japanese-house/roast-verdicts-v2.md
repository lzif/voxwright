## 1. Compound site-plan marking — FIX (merge + one factual correction)

- **Verdict: FIX.** The core is real and the dense set strengthens it: f0004 shows
  the full zone plan (tan + dark-brown + white outlines, cherry grove behind),
  f0019/f0053 show plan lines persisting on grass for unbuilt zones, and f0066
  shows the finished compound sitting on the planned lines. The v1 "approximate
  mapping" flag is genuinely resolved.
- **Correction 1 — the dimension numbers are not buildable.** The 33 / 53 / 40 /
  47 in f0004 are video-overlay typography (same bold white font + drop shadow
  as the "Japanese House" title card), floating above the terrain — not blocks
  in the world. "Annotate key dimensions on the plan" is therefore NOT a build
  step. Stage 3 must rewrite it as: work from a dimensioned plan (note the key
  spans before building); the reel's on-screen numbers are editing, not blocks.
  The numbers still support the principle (the builder planned with dimensions),
  but they cannot be a recipe step.
- **Correction 2 — do not number standalone: MERGE with French candidate 6.**
  The French reel's suggestion is CONFIRMED from this reel's evidence. Both
  candidates share one principle — decide the plan on the ground before building
  vertically; the ground drawing is the contract — and differ only at recipe
  level: Japanese = multi-zone plan for a compound, French = single-footprint
  outline for one complex building. Two numbers would bloat the guide with
  near-identical principles. Stage 3 writes ONE outline-first technique with
  two recipes, and must preserve the persistence distinction or the merge
  fails: Japanese zone lines PERSIST until their zone is built, then get
  absorbed into floors/paths; the French sacrificial outline is progressively
  REPLACED by foundation. Flattening that distinction would make the merged
  technique wrong about both reels. Cross-ref T2's corner-marking as the
  small-scale (single-house) version, not a duplicate.

## 5. Vermillion pavilion PAIR — KEEP (v2 correction confirmed)

- **Verdict: KEEP. The corrected rule holds.** f0053 shows both red-accented
  side halls flanking the main hall (left + right, symmetric), f0056 shows
  them again from the front garden, and f0066 confirms the twin pair in the
  finished showcase. V1's "exactly one pavilion" is refuted, and the v2 rule —

  that is rhetoric, not evidence. Write it as a preference: prefer the pair;
  a lone accent hall needs its own justification (e.g., it is the only
  secondary hall).

## 7. Forecourt garden — FIX (T8 extension, not standalone)

- **Verdict: FIX — fold into T8 as an extension bullet, do not number
  standalone.** The composition is real and verified (f0056: dirt court +
  round blue pond with white rim + central ornament + bamboo clusters +
  cherry trees + lantern posts; f0066 confirms in the finished showcase).
  But every element already exists in the guide: the pond is T8 formal water
  (basin-first, raised stone rim — the recipe matches T8's formal-water
  language verbatim), bamboo is T16, cherry trees are T10's backdrop, lantern
  posts are T6. The only new content is the arrangement (dirt court as
  arrival space, pond as center anchor, planting at edges) — and arrangement
  of existing techniques is exactly what a T8 extension bullet is for.
  Numbering it standalone would inflate the guide with a composition made of
  existing parts. Stage 3 writes it as a T8 bullet ("Forecourt garden") with
  the dirt-court + pond-anchor + edge-planting recipe and cross-refs to
  T16/T10/T6, keeping the "center open, planting at edges" guard.
- **Contrast with candidate 4 (kept standalone):** C4's sand-as-paving grid is
  a construction T8 does not cover; C7's pond is a construction T8 already
  covers. That is the line, and C7 falls on the extension side of it.

## 8. Lit display-niche wall — KEEP (as T7 variant, not standalone)

- **Verdict: KEEP as a T7 variant bullet, exactly as proposed.**
  Single-interior evidence, but the evidence is strong across three frames
  and two angles: f0031 (grid wall beside the dining area), f0033 (clearer:
  timber-framed grid of warm-lit cubbies), f0040 (same wall from the upper
  floor — wall-sized grid of lit squares above the dining furniture). This
  is not ordinary shelving: the regularity (wall-sized grid), the per-cubby
  warmth, and the display purpose separate it from T7's existing interior
  bullets — "recess, don't paste" is about sinking bookshelves/racks into
  walls, "storage walls" are about building walls from storage blocks.
  Neither covers a lit display grid.
- **Stage-3 guard (required, or the bullet collapses into T7's storage
  wall):** display, not storage — cubbies hold light + small props, not
  books/barrels. If the cubbies hold books it is T7's storage wall, not this.
  Note the single-interior caveat in the bullet; T7 bullets are routinely
  single-ref, so that alone does not disqualify it.
