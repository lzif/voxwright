**Numbers convention:** every dimension below is measured from the frames and marked ~ (approximate, ±1 block) unless stated otherwise. Dimensions the reel itself labels on screen (7×7, 15, 18) are reported as the reel's claims. Nothing here is block-counted from a schematic — there is none. ## Method and camera caveat

- Each step below names what is NEW vs the previous frame. Tags: **VISIBLE** = directly seen in the frames; **INFERRED** = order deduced (e.g. walls must precede the deck that sits on them, even though both appear in the same 1-second interval).
- **The camera is FIXED.** One aerial 3/4 viewpoint, the same two faces (front/entrance face + left face) in all 17 frames. The back and right faces are NEVER observed — any step touching those faces (window dressing, flower boxes, awnings) is assumed symmetric, and flagged where that assumption matters. This is the opposite of the Tall Mansion reel (orbiting camera); there is no angle-reveal risk here, but there is a permanent two-face blind spot.
- **The video starts at the bare site.** f_001 is the mound + patio with no wall at all, so the full sequence from site prep is observable — unlike the Tall Mansion reel, which starts mid-build. That is this reel's strength as a build record.
- **1-second granularity limit:** at 1 fps, "appears in the same interval" proves nothing about intent or sub-order inside that second. Steps that share a frame interval are marked INFERRED where the sub-order is deduced, not seen.
- No interior shots, no dusk showcase. Interior furnishing and lighting steps are out of scope — say so plainly below, not guessed.

## Mode assumption

The sequence is written as if built in SURVIVAL (access matters): the entrance stair goes in early so the builder can walk in instead of pillaring on dirt, the floor and ladder go in while the front is still open, and every step above floor 1 carries an access note (scaffold / dirt pillar / temp stairs / the deck as a work platform). In creative with flight the same order still works, but the access notes are unnecessary.

## The build, step by step

- **VISIBLE (f_001):** an irregular dirt mound ~2 high — patchwork of dirt / coarse dirt / mossy cobble / stone, no clean courses, ragged edge — with a stone-brick slab patio ~15×15 around it, its edge left ragged (slabs stepping out 1 block at irregular intervals), grass and flowers growing up to the edge. A player and a horse stand at the patio's edge (scale reference: the mound is ~2 blocks above the patio). Flower meadow all around; big custom trees and other village builds in the background.
- **INFERRED:** whether the mound is built terrain work or dressed natural terrain is UNRESOLVABLE — it is present from f_001 with no shaping visible. Either way it is site preparation, not landscape dressing: it is the ground the tower stands on. **Do NOT flatten it** — the mound is part of the composition (see candidate 3 in `01-alixxa-tower.md`).
- **Access:** ground level, no access needed.
- **Stage tags:** as-built footprint/grid (site prep); locked footprint/grid (agree — site prep belongs to the footprint stage).

- **INFERRED:** the footprint was marked (technique 2's corner-marking) before the ring went up, but the marking itself is not frame-visible. Do NOT write the overlay numbers as a build step — the numbers are the reel's dimension labels, and they are recorded as such in `01-alixxa-tower.md`.

### Step 3 — Floor + corner pillars + entrance stair + ground flower bed (f_004 → f_005)

### Step 4 — Walls up (f_005 → f_006)

The ladder is interior access for the upper courses: climb it to build the next lifts from inside. Whether the ladder runs the full shaft height is UNRESOLVABLE — only its lower section is ever visible.

## Deviations vs the locked process

- **D1 — dressing-per-stage (NOT a deviation, a confirmation):** the lower shaft is fully dressed (Steps 6–8) before the upper shaft starts (Step 9). Under the per-volume scope amendment (Tall Mansion, locked 2026-11-07), each stage is its own volume with its own shell → detail pass — so this order AGREES with the amended process. A builder who dressed everything at the very end would have to re-scaffold the lower shaft after the skirt roof went on.
- **D2 — site prep first (NOT a deviation):** the mound + patio (Step 0) come before any wall. The locked process's "landscape last" refers to dressing/planting; the mound is terrain-shaping (site prep, footprint stage) and the meadow flowers are pre-existing terrain. No amendment needed.
- **D3 — floor + access stair early (access, not interior):** the floor and entrance stair go in at Step 3 while the front is still open, so the builder walks in and builds up from inside. Same rule as the Tall Mansion reel. The locked process's "interior only after shell closed" does not cover access circulation.
- **D4 — rim railing before the upper shaft (rule, not deviation):** rail the terrace rim (Step 8) before the upper shaft rises (Step 9), or the railing has to be installed leaning over the shaft afterward.

## Verdict

No process amendment is needed from this reel. It confirms the per-volume scope amendment: two volumes, each with its own shell → detail pass, crown last on the second volume. The build is a clean single-tower sequence — footprint → ring → floor → walls → deck → dress → second shaft → skirt → dress → crown — with no macro-order contradiction. The one process-relevant finding is D1 (dressing-per-stage), which is the amendment working as intended, not a new deviation.
