## Process-level finding (decision for Luki, not a silent fix)

Two lenses independently conclude the locked staged process (2026-11-01, generalized from ONE reel — TrixyBlox Ruined Temple, a single-volume build) fails its second test: this reel contradicts its macro-order at every joint.

- Wing-first is the EFFICIENT order: the low wing is a work platform and a proportion reference; signature-first means scaffolding from the ground plus blind proportions. D1 is an efficient deviation, not a defect.
- "Signature alone first" conflates a filming choice (hook the viewer in 3 seconds) with a building choice.
- D5 is access, not interior: floor + stairs while the front wall is open is efficient — walk in, don't haul materials through a finished 1-wide door into a closed dark box.
- Proposed amendment (for Luki to lock or reject): "stage discipline applies per volume; signature-first applies when the build's identity is a single volume." The locked process needs a revision, or at least a scope note limiting it to single-volume builds.
